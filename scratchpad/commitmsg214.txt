release v1.0.214 — cancelled invoices no longer count as unpaid

User-reported: a cancelled invoice still showed on a customer's unpaid list.

Root cause: DB.getCustomerBalance() has always excluded cancelled invoices, but SIX
other places computed "unpaid/outstanding" by re-filtering on customerId alone and
so silently kept them. The customer card contradicted itself — the ค้างสุทธิ money
badge excluded a voided invoice while the "N ค้างชำระ" count and the red chip list
right beside it still counted it. CLAUDE.md already documents the convention these
sites were violating: cancelled invoices stay visible in list views but never count
toward money totals.

Two of the six were customer-facing and two touched real money:
- copyOverdueMsg() — the reminder text copied to send to the customer, which would
  demand payment for a voided invoice ("2 ใบ รวม 8,277.00" instead of "1 ใบ 500.00")
- showOutstandingPreview() ×2 (customers.html + payments.html) — the printed
  ใบแจ้งยอดค้างชำระ statement handed to the customer
- _overpayOutstanding() — offered cancelled invoices as targets when cutting an
  overpayment, i.e. would allocate REAL credit onto a bill that no longer exists
- the sort-by-balance comparator, and getPayTermsViolations(), which raised false
  credit-limit warnings by counting voided bills toward maxBills/maxDays

Fix: new DB.getActiveInvoicesByCustomer(custId) is the single list every money
calculation now draws from, so this can't drift apart again. Per explicit user
decision the customer card hides cancelled invoices completely — the "N ใบกำกับ"
count and "ใบล่าสุด" date exclude them too (the void was the newest invoice in
testing, so it was posing as the customer's latest activity). They remain visible
on invoices.html with their ยกเลิก badge, as the convention intends.

test-cancelled-excluded-from-unpaid.js adds 22 assertions driving the REAL code out
of db.js/customers.html/payments.html. Discrimination was verified, not assumed: with
the filter removed, 15 of the 22 fail with exactly the reported symptoms (statement
lists the void, overpay offers it as a credit target, reminder says 2 ใบ 8,277.00,
false เกินกำหนด violation) — the 7 that still pass are the no-cancelled regression
cases, which is correct. Full regression of all fifteen suites (269 assertions) green
before and after the version bump.
