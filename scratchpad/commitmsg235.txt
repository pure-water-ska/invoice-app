release v1.0.235 — retire ราคากลาง (ทุกลูกค้า); convert the 11 in use to customer prices

User request: remove the ทุกลูกค้า prices — "which customer prices refer to standard
prices, just keep its value". Asked which customers, the user chose ONLY the
customer × product × delivery combinations actually invoiced at a standard price (11 on
live data, 7 customers) over giving every active customer every fallback (2,769 new
rules, 3,280 → 5,990). Shown a mockup, approved.

Live data (read-only): 59 standard rules across 30 products (30 รับหน้าโรงงาน,
29 จัดส่ง); 1 active customer with no prices at all (มัสยิดกลาง ปริก); 12 invoice lines
on 10 invoices for 6 customers used a standard price in the last 90 days; the product
"default price" field is 0 on all 32 products, so it was never the issue.

pricing.html:
  - A one-time, admin-only card lists the conversions (_stdConversions: non-cancelled
    invoices of existing customers, for products the customer has no own price for on
    that delivery method or on every method), with the last billed price and invoice,
    and an editable value defaulting to the standard price. A standard price whose
    newest history entry disagrees is flagged (_stdLostSave) — live, ถุงขวด 830(ขุ่น)
    เกรด A shows 92 but aing saved 70 on 8 Aug (one of the lost saves fixed in
    v1.0.234). Values typed in the card survive re-renders.
  - retireStandardPrices(): admin only, Utils.confirm, rejects empty/negative values;
    adds each conversion as the customer's own rule (tier fields copied, notify date
    not, new id) with a price-history entry noted แปลงจากราคากลาง, deletes every
    standard rule, all in ONE savePricing — which the per-rule sync from v1.0.234
    uploads as individual adds and deletes — and logs ยกเลิกราคากลาง. The card then
    disappears. It is NOT run by this release: an admin presses it after updating.
  - The customer picker no longer offers "— ทุกลูกค้า (ราคากลาง) —"; savePrice requires
    a customer. Placeholder, banner ("ที่ยังไม่มีราคา — ต้องตั้งราคาก่อนออกใบกำกับ"),
    the empty-state message and the priority note no longer promise a standard price.

db.js: DB.getPriceAsOf — which invoices use — fell back to standard-price HISTORY,
which keeps every old standard price forever, so deleting the rules alone would have kept
charging a retired price. It now falls back to it only while a standard rule for that
product still exists: unchanged until the conversion, no fallback after. getPrice /
getPricingRule keep their chain; with no standard rules they find none. _seedPricing
(brand-new installs only) is untouched — its local seeds are never uploaded.

Verified live in the preview on real data without pressing convert: the card showed
7 customers / 11 items / 59 to delete with the flagged row; the picker had no
ทุกลูกค้า and both fields were marked required; rules, history and the upload queue were
unchanged afterwards.

test-standard-price-retire.js — 49 assertions driving the REAL page functions and the
REAL DB.getPriceAsOf. Mutation-checked, thirteen breaks, all caught (a first run exposed
a sample where the newest invoice came first, so "newest wins" was untested; reordered).
test-pricing-no-price-customers.js now expects the new wording. CLAUDE.md documents the
retirement. Full regression of all thirty-one suites (905 assertions) green.
