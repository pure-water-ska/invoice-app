Two user-requested changes to the customer list.

1. Status tabs — ทั้งหมด / ใช้งาน / ระงับ, with counts.

Suspension was already an ENFORCED state everywhere else: invoice-create blocks a new
invoice for a suspended customer in three separate places and shows a warning banner,
pricing warns when setting their prices, and the flag carries a reason, timestamp, author
and history log. But customers.html only dimmed the card and dropped it into the same
24-per-page grid, with a sort dropdown offering name and balance only — no way to filter
by status, so a suspended customer could sit anywhere across the pages.

ทั้งหมด is the default deliberately. Suspension usually FOLLOWS unpaid debt, so an
active-only default would hide exactly the accounts most worth chasing; the ระงับ tab
doubles as a "who owes us and is cut off" view, balance badges intact. Within ทั้งหมด

inside each group. Counts are of the current search result, computed before the status
filter — otherwise each tab would report itself as the total.

2. Unpaid-invoice chips capped at 5, expandable in place.

Every unpaid invoice rendered as a chip with no cap, so a customer with 20 outstanding
produced a card tall enough to stretch its whole grid row (h-100 cards in a row g-3). The
list was already sorted by remaining amount descending, so the first five are the largest
debts.

The toggle reads "อีก N ใบ · ฿X" rather than a bare "see more": without the amount, a
customer with 6 unpaid looks identical to one with 30 and real debt quietly leaves the
screen — the same trap as the cancelled-invoice and stale-record work earlier this week.
Expansion is per customer and held outside render(), so it survives a re-render from a
search keystroke or a sync event instead of snapping shut mid-read.
