release v1.0.221 — payment summary print now reconciles

User-reported: over/under-paid amounts moved onto another invoice should appear on the
printed payment summary. It turned out to be worse than an omission — the sheet did not
ADD UP, on a document handed to the customer.

The payment rows print each payment's RAW amount, while ชำระแล้ว uses
getInvoicePaidAmount() → effectivePaymentAmount(), which subtracts whatever was allocated
to other invoices. So an invoice whose overpayment was cut elsewhere printed a ฿10,000 row
above a ฿8,000 "ชำระแล้ว" with ฿2,000 unexplained and the status still reading ✓ ชำระครบ.

Three things separate the rows from the net figure; each now gets its own summary line,
and each only prints when it applies, so an ordinary invoice is unchanged:
  • หัก ตัดไปใบอื่น — allocated away (subtracts)
  • ยกยอดไปใบใหม่ (ไม่ใช่เงินสด) — the customer's OWN old debt moved onto a new invoice.
    It settles this invoice, so it counts toward paid, but it is NOT money they handed
    over and must not sit inside a "รับเงิน" total.
  • เช็ครอขึ้นเงิน (ยังไม่นับ) — printed in the rows but deliberately excluded from paid.
    Not part of the original request, but without it the new reconciling column still
    would not have reconciled whenever an un-cleared cheque was present.
When any of these apply the cash line is relabelled รวมรับเงินจริง and a ชำระแล้ว (สุทธิ)
line closes the arithmetic: cash + carryForward − allocated = paid, by construction.

Each allocation also prints under the payment it came from, naming the destination
invoice ("↳ ตัดไปใบ 030869-003 ฿2,000.00") from the allocations[] audit trail, so the
customer can trace their money rather than just seeing a smaller total. Carry-forward rows
are greyed and marked "↳ ไม่ใช่การรับเงิน" on the row itself.

Also fixes a pre-existing bug the rendering check exposed: methodDetail() fell back to
p.notes for any method outside เงินสด/โอน/เช็ค while the caller appends p.notes again, so
the note printed twice — which in practice meant exactly the carry-forward rows
("ยกยอดค้างชำระไปใบกำกับ X | ยกยอดค้างชำระไปใบกำกับ X"). The fallback now returns ''.

test-payment-summary-print.js adds 32 assertions driving the real extracted breakdown
against real DB predicates. The load-bearing ones assert the identity
cash + carryForward − allocated = paid holds in every combination: allocation only,
carry-forward only, pending cheque only, all three at once, multiple allocations from one
payment, and an ordinary invoice. Plus the cleared-cheque case, the conditional rendering
of each line, and the methodDetail de-duplication. Rendered print verified in a browser
before and after the duplication fix. Full regression of all twenty suites (418
assertions) green before and after the bump.
