release v1.0.224 — rounding buttons only on genuinely over/under-paid invoices

User-reported, and correct: v1.0.223 gated the ตัดเศษ button on `(total - paid) > 0.005`,
which is ALSO true of a wholly unpaid invoice — its shortfall is simply its full value. So
every pending bill offered to write itself off, including a ฿28,477 one that had never been
touched. With no threshold cap (the user's explicit choice) that is the worst thing the
button could have offered.

Rounding is for an invoice that HAS been paid but whose amount doesn't match — partial or
over. The down button now additionally requires `paid > 0.005`, and openRoundModal
re-checks it so calling the handler directly can't bypass the row condition either.

Verified against the real extracted condition:
  wholly unpaid ฿28,477   → no button      (was: offered to write off ฿28,477)
  partial, ฿0.45 short    → ตัดเศษ
  partial, ฿5,000 short   → ตัดเศษ         (no cap, by user decision)
  paid exactly            → no button
  overpaid ฿0.45          → ปัดขึ้น
  cancelled               → no button

test-payment-rounding.js 40 → 41: the row condition must require money actually paid, and
the modal must re-check it. Full regression of all twenty suites (430 assertions) green
before and after the version bump.
