release v1.0.223 — round down (write off) / round up on payments, and a real income split

User request: clear the penny remainders customers never pay, "without record as income".
Their data is full of them — every carry-forward transfer on file was ฿0.05–฿1.20, i.e.
the carry-forward feature was being used as a makeshift write-off.

The two directions are deliberately ASYMMETRIC, because the money differs (the user's own
framing, and it is the correct accounting):
  down (ตัดเศษ)  — the shortfall was NEVER received. Forgiving it settles the invoice but
                   must not create income. DB.writeOffRemainder writes a non-cash payment.
  up   (ปัดขึ้น) — the excess WAS received and is already a real payment, so it stays
                   income. DB.roundUpInvoice writes NO payment at all; the invoice total
                   rises to meet what was actually paid, closing the balance.
Round-up applies to every current page (each page carries the same whole-invoice total, so
touching only page 1 would leave them disagreeing) and skips stale duplicate pages.

This exposed a real pre-existing bug. `carryForward` appeared ONLY in db.js and
invoice-create.html — nothing in reports excluded it — so every non-cash carry-forward has
been silently counted as revenue. Fixed generally rather than for write-offs alone:
  • DB.isNonCashPayment(p)          — carryForward OR writeOff
  • DB.getInvoiceCashReceived(...)  — paid minus non-cash; use for INCOME
  • DB.getInvoicePaidAmount(...)    — unchanged; use for SETTLEMENT
Reports' headline and per-group totals now use the income figure, and the payment-method
report drops non-cash rows. The dashboard is untouched on purpose — all three of its uses
are settlement (status/remaining), not income.

The drill-down detail view keeps settlement totals, because its per-row badges ask "is this
invoice closed?" and the two would otherwise contradict each other; instead the non-cash
portion is broken out as its own badge, so it is never presented as money received while
the figures still reconcile.

No threshold cap, by explicit user decision after the risk was raised. Kept instead:
payment_edit gate, a confirm naming the exact amount and its consequences, an optional
reason, a re-entrancy lock on the confirm button, an activity-log entry per action, and
DB.logError on failure.

test-payment-rounding.js adds 40 assertions on the real DB: both directions end to end,
the settlement-vs-income split across cash/carry-forward/write-off/cancelled/pending-cheque
together, allocated-away overpayments still deducted, multi-page round-up, stale pages left
alone, and the reports/dashboard/UI wiring. test-reports-payment-filter.js grows 7 → 10
covering the new non-cash exclusion.

Note on process: patching three files with a Python script silently rewrote them to CRLF
(text-mode write on Windows) — every line showed as changed and it broke a test's
extraction. Caught by the full regression, converted back to LF, and the diff is now 210
insertions / 6 deletions as intended. Full regression of all twenty suites (429 assertions)
green before and after the version bump.
